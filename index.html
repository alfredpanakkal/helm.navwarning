Live NAVAREA Warnings for Passage Planning

Helm.Warning is a free, modern maritime safety information (MSI) cartography service providing a live NAVAREA warnings map for watchkeeping officers, navigators, and passage planners. In-force navigational hazard notices and coastal warnings are aggregated directly from official hydrographic authorities across all 21 NAVAREA regions worldwide (I through XXI, including Arctic areas XVII, XVIII, XIX, XX, and XXI). No login or account creation is required to inspect in-force bulletins, danger areas, firing exercises, drifting hazards, and aids-to-navigation discrepancies.

Navigational warnings communicate time-critical ocean hazards promulgated under the World-Wide Navigational Warning Service (WWNWS), jointly coordinated by the International Maritime Organization (IMO) and the International Hydrographic Organization (IHO). Helm.Warning parses structured coordinates, radius circles, danger polygons, and AtoN notices from 14 verified hydrographic feeds—including the United Kingdom Hydrographic Office (UKHO), National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA), Service Hydrographique et Océanographique de la Marine (SHOM), Australian Maritime Safety Authority (AMSA), and Japan Coast Guard (JCG)—rendering georeferenced bulletins directly on interactive OpenStreetMap, CARTO, and OpenSeaMap nautical charts.

Official Regional Coordinators & Arctic Coverage

Active coverage spans the North and South Atlantic, Mediterranean, Baltic Sea, Indian Ocean, Red Sea, Persian Gulf, East Asia, Pacific Ocean, and the five Arctic navigational zones (NAVAREA XVII–XVIII Canadian Coast Guard, XIX Norwegian Coastal Administration, and XX–XXI Russian Northern Sea Route).

  • NAVAREA I: North Atlantic & North Sea · UKHO (United Kingdom)
  • NAVAREA II: East Atlantic & Biscay · SHOM (France)
  • NAVAREA III: Mediterranean & Black Sea · IHM (Spain)
  • NAVAREA IV: West Atlantic & Gulf of Mexico · NGA (United States)
  • NAVAREA V: South Atlantic (Brazil) · DHN (Brazil)
  • NAVAREA VI: South Atlantic (Argentina) · SHN (Argentina)
  • NAVAREA VII: South Africa & Southern Ocean · SAN (South Africa)
  • NAVAREA VIII: North Indian Ocean · INHD (India)
  • NAVAREA IX: Red Sea, Persian Gulf & Arabian Sea · PNHD (Pakistan)
  • NAVAREA X: Australia & Coral Sea · AMSA (Australia)
  • NAVAREA XI: East Asia, South China Sea & Japan · JCG (Japan)
  • NAVAREA XII: East Pacific & US West Coast · NGA (United States)
  • NAVAREA XIII: NW Pacific & Sea of Okhotsk · Rosmorrechflot (Russia)
  • NAVAREA XIV: South Pacific & Tasman Sea · LINZ (New Zealand)
  • NAVAREA XV: Southeast Pacific · SHOA (Chile)
  • NAVAREA XVI: Southeast Pacific · DHN (Peru)
  • NAVAREA XVII & XVIII: Arctic Canada · CCG (Canada)
  • NAVAREA XIX: Arctic Norway · NCA (Norway)
  • NAVAREA XX & XXI: Arctic Russia · Rosmorrechflot (Russia)

Notice to Mariners: Helm.Warning is an automated situational decision-support cartography tool. It is not an official substitute for SOLAS carriage requirements, GMDSS equipment, NAVTEX receivers, or Inmarsat-C / Iridium SafetyNET broadcasts. Master mariners remain responsible for maintaining statutory bridge watches and consulting official government MSI broadcasts.
