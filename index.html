About HELM.WARNING

HELM.WARNING is a free interactive map of maritime navigational warnings, built to support situational awareness and passage planning.

Warnings from official NAVAREA coordinators are plotted on a map to help watchkeepers identify potential hazards, including danger areas, firing exercises, drifting hazards, and subsea operations.

Explore warnings across all 21 NAVAREA regions, including the Arctic. No account is required.

Safety Disclaimer

HELM.WARNING is a situational-awareness tool, not an official source of Maritime Safety Information (MSI).

Warnings may be delayed, incomplete, or incorrectly plotted. Always verify warnings against official NAVTEX, SafetyNET, or issuing-authority broadcasts before acting.

Helm.Warning is a situational-awareness tool. It shows a convenience copy of NAVAREA and other maritime safety warnings gathered from public sources. It is not an official source of Maritime Safety Information.

The map can lag behind the original broadcasts, miss warnings, or show them incorrectly. Always check the official broadcast (NAVTEX, SafetyNET, or the issuing authority) before relying on any warning.

Official broadcasts and the Master's judgment take priority over anything shown here. Under SOLAS, responsibility for the safe navigation of the vessel stays with the Master. Helm.Warning must not be used as the sole or primary source for passage planning or navigation decisions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about navigational warnings, data coverage, and using HELM.WARNING.

Is HELM.WARNING free?

Yes. HELM.WARNING is completely free. There is no account, sign-up, or paid tier. The map, regional pages, and warning details are available to everyone.

What is a NAVAREA warning?

A navigational warning is a maritime safety message about hazards or activities that may affect safe navigation.

Examples include firing exercises, drifting hazards, subsea operations, offshore installations, and disabled vessels.

The world's oceans are divided into 21 NAVAREA regions, each with a designated coordinator responsible for issuing, updating, and cancelling warnings under the World-Wide Navigational Warning Service (WWNWS).

Where does the data come from?

HELM.WARNING collects publicly available warnings from official NAVAREA coordinators and maritime authorities.

Sources include the UK Hydrographic Office (UKHO), National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA), India's hydrographic authorities, Japan Coast Guard, and Australian Maritime Safety Authority (AMSA).

Original bulletin text is preserved alongside parsed warning information.

How fresh is the data?

Verified automated feeds are updated according to their ingestion schedules. Many are checked daily, while some may be updated more frequently.

Warnings can be cancelled or superseded at any time. Always check the issuing authority for the latest information.

Can I use it for passage planning?

HELM.WARNING can support situational awareness and passage planning, but it is not an official MSI distributor.

It may contain delayed, incomplete, or incorrectly plotted warnings. It does not replace official broadcasts, ECDIS, or required navigational publications.

Why does my region show no warnings?

The coordinator may have no warnings currently in force, or the region may not have a verified automated feed.

Some regions rely on reviewed manual entry. A region with no displayed warnings is not necessarily free of navigational hazards.

Does it work at sea, on a slow connection?

HELM.WARNING is designed to remain lightweight and usable on limited connections.

However, retrieving live warnings requires an internet connection. Save or copy the official bulletins you need before losing connectivity.

Live NAVAREA Warnings for Passage Planning

Helm.Warning is a free, modern maritime safety information (MSI) cartography service providing a live NAVAREA warnings map for watchkeeping officers, navigators, and passage planners. In-force navigational hazard notices and coastal warnings are aggregated directly from official hydrographic authorities across all 21 NAVAREA regions worldwide (I through XXI, including Arctic areas XVII, XVIII, XIX, XX, and XXI). No login or account creation is required to inspect in-force bulletins, danger areas, firing exercises, drifting hazards, and aids-to-navigation discrepancies.

Navigational warnings communicate time-critical ocean hazards promulgated under the World-Wide Navigational Warning Service (WWNWS), jointly coordinated by the International Maritime Organization (IMO) and the International Hydrographic Organization (IHO). Helm.Warning parses structured coordinates, radius circles, danger polygons, and AtoN notices from 14 verified hydrographic feeds—including the United Kingdom Hydrographic Office (UKHO), National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA), Service Hydrographique et Océanographique de la Marine (SHOM), Australian Maritime Safety Authority (AMSA), and Japan Coast Guard (JCG)—rendering georeferenced bulletins directly on interactive OpenStreetMap, CARTO, and OpenSeaMap nautical charts.
