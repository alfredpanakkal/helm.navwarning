NAVAREA Source Registry

Helm.Warning tracks navigational warnings for the 21 NAVAREA ocean regions of the World-Wide Navigational Warning Service (WWNWS), run jointly by the International Maritime Organization (IMO) and the International Hydrographic Organization (IHO). Every region has one designated area coordinator — a national hydrographic office or maritime authority — responsible for issuing, updating and cancelling its warnings. Select any region below to open its regional page with the live in-force warnings and the original bulletins.

Regions: 21 · Coverage: global, including the Arctic · Feeds: verified official coordinator broadcasts

About this registry

Helm.Warning ingests each region from verified official coordinator feeds and plots the parsed warnings on the live map. Where no plain-text feed is available for a region, its warnings are entered through the reviewed admin pipeline rather than an unofficial source — coverage for a region therefore reflects what the coordinator actually broadcasts.

Helm.Warning is a convenience copy of these broadcasts, not an official source of Maritime Safety Information. Always verify against official channels (NAVTEX, Inmarsat SafetyNET) as required by SOLAS — see the about and disclaimer page for the full safety notice.

Situational awareness only. Not a substitute for GMDSS, NAVTEX or SafetyNET broadcasts. Official broadcasts and the Master’s judgment always take priority.
