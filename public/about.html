About Helm.Warning

What is Helm.Warning?

Helm.Warning is a free interactive map that displays live NAVAREA navigational warnings to support maritime situational awareness and passage planning. Active warnings are plotted on nautical charts with danger areas, safety zones, and hazard points — no login or account required.

Explore the 21 NAVAREA areas & warnings

Data Sources

Warnings are aggregated from official NAVAREA coordinators operating under the World-Wide Navigational Warning Service (WWNWS), jointly coordinated by the International Maritime Organization (IMO) and the International Hydrographic Organization (IHO). Coverage spans all 21 NAVAREA regions worldwide, including the five Arctic areas (XVII–XXI). Data is refreshed daily from verified hydrographic authority feeds such as the UKHO, NGA, SHOM, AMSA, and the Japan Coast Guard.

Every NAVAREA ocean region has one designated area coordinator — a national hydrographic office or maritime authority — responsible for issuing, updating and cancelling its warnings. Select any region below to open its regional page with the live in-force warnings and the original bulletins.

Helm.Warning ingests each region and plots the parsed warnings on the live map. Every card carries an ingestion status: green = automated ingestion from a verified official feed; amber = not reliable, the source does not permit automatic crawling (bot-protected portal or no accessible publication endpoint), so data may be stale; grey = no automated feed yet, warnings entered through the reviewed admin pipeline rather than an unofficial source. Regions with a verified source portal also link to it directly below the card.

Important Safety Disclaimer

Helm.Warning is a situational-awareness tool. It shows a convenience copy of NAVAREA and other maritime safety warnings gathered from public sources. It is not an official source of Maritime Safety Information.

The map can lag behind the original broadcasts, miss warnings, or show them incorrectly. Always check the official broadcast (NAVTEX, SafetyNET, or the issuing authority) before relying on any warning.

Official broadcasts and the Master's judgment take priority over anything shown here. Under SOLAS, responsibility for the safe navigation of the vessel stays with the Master. Helm.Warning must not be used as the sole or primary source for passage planning or navigation decisions.

Display & Watch Themes

Helm.Warning follows the watch. Pick the theme that suits your station and time of day — your choice is saved on this device and applies across the site, including the live map.

Red watch is intentionally dim. These colours are calculated, not tested on a bridge display — try it at night on your own device and adjust screen brightness until it is comfortable. Severity colours keep the same meaning in every theme: a Critical warning always looks Critical.
