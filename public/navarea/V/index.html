About NAVAREA V

NAVAREA V is one of the 21 ocean regions of the World-Wide Navigational Warning Service (WWNWS), coordinated jointly by the International Maritime Organization (IMO) and the International Hydrographic Organization (IHO). The designated area coordinator is the Diretoria de Hidrografia e Navegacao (DHN, Brazil), which issues and cancels the region’s navigational warnings. Helm.Warning aggregates these broadcasts daily from verified official feeds and plots them on the live map.

Helm.Warning is a convenience copy of these broadcasts, not an official source of Maritime Safety Information. Always verify against official channels (NAVTEX, Inmarsat SafetyNET) as required by SOLAS — see the about and disclaimer page for the full safety notice.
